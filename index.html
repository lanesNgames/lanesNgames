Success measures for leaders

Leading indicators

  • 80% of target users complete the trail stops.
  • Scenario retry rate drops after reinforcement huddles.
  • Confidence and adherence scores improve by role.

Behavior indicators

  • Forecast submissions follow the standard sequence.
  • Budget owners document assumptions before approval.
  • Exception handling uses the shared escalation path.

Business indicators

  • Fewer budget rework cycles and correction tickets.
  • Reduced variance caused by process misses.
  • Higher manager confidence in cycle readiness.
